### [3] Abstract (Makale Özeti)

> 💡 **Not:** SSRN web arayüzünde paragraflar net ve okunabilir şekilde aşağıdaki tam metni yapıştırınız:

```text
Contemporary Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED) and Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITS) face a dual infrastructural and pedagogical crisis. Infrastructurally, cloud-hosted Large Language Models (LLMs) and dense Deep Knowledge Tracing (DKT) networks impose severe memory-bandwidth bottlenecks (4–80 GB VRAM), high latencies (>300 ms), privacy risks, and stochastic hallucinations. Pedagogically, systemic educational transformation frameworks grounded in chaos and complexity theory (Reigeluth, 2008) posit that learner empowerment and cognitive disequilibrium are prerequisites for self-organization; however, unconstrained self-directed learning consistently triggers the Saturn School Paradox (Bennett & King, 1991; Reigeluth, 2008), where autonomous self-direction without mathematical boundary damping collapses time-on-task and mastery retention.

We introduce Procedural Fractal Pedagogy (PFP), realized via the open-source WerreduR cognitive reflex engine. PFP formalizes Vygotsky's Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD) and Reigeluth's disequilibrium corridor as an explicit Observer Horizon Corridor anchored at sub-boundary resonance shoulder loci X_upper/lower = (0.25, ±0.18) along the boundary of the Mandelbrot set (∂M, z_{n+1} = z_n^2 + c). Individualized adaptive curricula are synthesized on demand from a 24-byte student coordinate seed Θ = (c_x, c_y, zoom) with O(1) constant memory complexity (0 Bytes persistent VRAM). To eliminate Saturn School learner drift and interior rote stagnation, PFP deploys an Information-Theoretic Semantic Token Damping Filter (T_desc = 0.045), a Biomimetic Perturbed Jump Operator (Ω_tunneling), and a Multi-Scale Harmonic Tripod kernel. Refuting the classical Fallacy of Erasure in scalar grading, student assessment is formalized as a unitary, non-dissipative error-kernel invariant (K_error ≅ I_3 = {0, 3, 6} ⊂ Z/9Z) under TAMAMe Horizon Complementarity B(t) + S(t) = 1, machine-verified in Lean 4 with zero sorry axioms.

Across N = 1,000 self-directed trajectories (T = 120 instructional cycles), unconstrained Saturn School self-direction collapses to 11.68% ± 0.31% time-on-task, whereas WerreduR achieves 94.19% ± 0.14% time-on-task (+82.51 pp vs. Saturn, p = 4.86e-11; +5.23 pp over Cloud LLMs, d = 1.54), 89.24% ± 0.13% ZPD residence, a 339.4x suppression in cognitive burnout stress (1.29 vs. 437.84), and a median decision triage latency of 2.32 ± 0.01 ms (134.5x faster than Cloud LLMs) with zero persistent VRAM.
```
